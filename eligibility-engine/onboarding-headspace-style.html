🧘 Design principles (Headspace / Headway)

  • Typography is the hero — 28pt questions, not speech bubbles
  • Single-color accent gradient on the emotional word (basics, journey, else)
  • Warm background gradients shift per step (lavender → peach → mint) to signal progress emotionally
  • Soft blurred blobs instead of mascots — decorative warmth without competing for attention
  • No close buttons-as-arrows. Proper × circle for cancel, clear progress bar for navigation
  • Fields labeled in small uppercase caps — understated, not shouty
  • Rounded 16px field radius, thick 1.5px border, soft hover glow
  • Staggered slide-up entrance (50ms between each field) — calm, intentional
  • Sticky bottom button area with subtle fade — content never touches button
  • Navy button with aggressive shadow — the one thing that really pops
