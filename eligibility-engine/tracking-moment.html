🎯 The copy

"Prep is the hard part. The dates? That's on us."

Two sentences. First acknowledges their effort. Second makes a promise. Separated by a question mark that creates conversational rhythm — reads like a friend, not a product.

🧘 Why Headspace / Headway loved this frame

  • Acknowledges effort before making the promise — the emotional handshake lands first
  • Question mark rhythm breaks the monotone of feature copy
  • "That's on us" transfers mental load — no guilt, no sell
  • Specific words (prep, dates) not vague (updates, reminders)

📍 Two placements, same copy

  • First-time nudge — shown once on the topmost card to teach what Track Now does. Warm yellow pill.
  • Helper text — always visible below every Track Now button. Subtle accent card with the copy highlighted.
  • After tracking — copy changes to "You're set. We'll tap you on the shoulder when it's time."
